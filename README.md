Metroid is an open-source software developed to simulate streaks and trails in astronomical images that are caused by orbital objects such as satellites and space debris.

Yes, it is a mangled backronym. Yes, Nintendo may come after me and make me change the name. But until then I like to name my projects after my favorite games, movies, and artists.
